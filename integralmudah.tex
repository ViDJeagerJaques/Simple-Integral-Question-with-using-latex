\[1. \text{Apabila nilai dari}\int_{1}^{3} (3x+5) \, dx + \int_{2}^{b} (2x-3) \, dx=28 \]
\[\text{dengan b>0, maka nilai dari b berapa?} \]
\[\text{Penyelesaian:}\]
\[\frac{3x^2}{2} + 5x + \frac{2x^2}{2} -3x = 28\]\
\[\frac{3x^2}{2} + 5x + \frac{\cancel{2x^2}}{\cancel{2}} -3x = 28\]
\[\frac{3x^2}{2} + 5x + x^2-3x = 28\]
\[\left[\frac{27}{2}+15\right] - \left|\frac{3}{2}+5\right| + \left[b^2-3b\right] -\left[4-6\right] = 28\]
\[\frac{27}{2}+15 - \frac{3}{2}-5 + b^2 - 3b -4 + 6 = 28 \]
\[\frac{27}{2}+\frac{30}{2} - \frac{3}{2} -\frac{10}{2} = \frac{\cancel{44}}{\cancel2} = 22 \]
\[22+b^2 -3b - 4 + 6 = 28\]
\[b^2 - 3b + 24 = 28\]
\[b^2 -3b +24 - 28 = 0\]
\[b^2 - 3b - 4 = 0\]
\[\left(b-4\right) \left(b+1\right)\]
\[ b = 4 \vee b = -1\]
\[\text{karena b>0, maka akar-akar b yang memenuhi hanya b=4.}\]
\[\boxed{\text{Maka nilai dari b adalah 4.}}\]

2. Diketahui fungsi \(f(x) = 3^{2x-4}\). Jika \(f^{-1}(p) = 3\) maka p = 
\begin{itemize}
    \item[(A)] 5
    \item[(B)] 6
    \item[(C)] 7
    \item[(D)] 8
    \item[(E)] 9
\end{itemize}
Penyelesaian : 
\\
\noindent\textbf{Ingat bentuk umum fungsi invers!!}
\[f (x) = y \Rightarrow f^{-1}(y) = x\]
Maka : 
\[f^{-1} (p) = 3 \rightarrow f (3) = p\]
Masukkan nilai 3 ke fungsi \(f(x)^{2x=4}\) 
\[f(3) = 3^{2(3)-4}\]
\[f(3) = 3^{6-4}\]
\[f(3) = 3^2\]
\[f(3) = 9\]
\textbf{Jawaban: (E) 9}
